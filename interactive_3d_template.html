Market activity explorer

Every dot is one market snapshot.

A dot represents one row from the source files: one timestamp, one Bid or Ask side, one strike level, the ES futures price, and the option-risk values calculated for that moment. The dot is a recorded market condition, not necessarily an executed trade.

1. Read one dot

One dot means one timestamped snapshot. Nearby dots had similar values. A dense area means many snapshots looked alike.

2. Read the axes

The selected metrics decide left-to-right, front-to-back, and bottom-to-top position. Change one axis at a time when comparing patterns.

3. Read the color

Color can separate Bid from Ask, separate source files, or show whether a metric is low or high. Split colors usually mean different behavior.
